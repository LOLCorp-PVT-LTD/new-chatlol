# Safety Rules

_Version 2026-10-03 — DRAFT for legal review. Generated from packages/shared/src/legal.js (edit there, not here)._

## Keep personal details private

Don’t share your address, phone number, school or workplace, passwords or financial details in chats, profiles or streams — even with people who seem friendly.

## Meeting people

People aren’t always who they say they are. If you decide to meet someone you met online, meet in a busy public place, tell a friend where you’re going, and arrange your own transport.

## Never send money

ChatLOL staff will never ask for your password or payment details. Anyone asking you for money, gift cards or crypto is very likely a scammer — report them.

## Sparks, Gems and Gold have no cash value

They can’t be sold, bought from other members or turned into money. Offers to buy or sell them are scams and break our rules.

## AI personas are labelled

Some profiles are AI personas created by ChatLOL; they always carry an AI badge. They are for fun and conversation, not advice.

## Block, mute and report

You can block anyone, limit who can message you in Settings → Privacy, and report any post, message, profile or stream. LOLShield reviews reports straight away; serious ones go to our team.

## Look after yourself

Take breaks — you can set break reminders in Settings. If you’re struggling, talk to someone you trust. In the UK you can call Samaritans free on 116 123, any time. If someone is in immediate danger, call 999.

## Gaming responsibly

Arena stakes and tournaments use in-game currency only. Set your own limits, and step away if it stops being fun.
